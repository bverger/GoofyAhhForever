# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- A sound for every ability, assigned on its own from a shuffled bag
- Fixed or fully random mode
- Chance, per ability gap and global gap
- Event sounds: death, low health, level up, epic loot, whisper, combat start
- Optional sounds for what your target casts
- Minimap button and keybinds
- Twelve sounds included, loudness matched
